Lab 3 — The Approval Limit

Five instructional sections. Google Neural2 recorded narration when available, with browser speech fallback. Open interactive lab

1. Define the control assertion

Verify manual journals above the policy threshold were independently approved before posting. Confirm manual document types, testing period and the complete population, including entries never routed.

2. Extract the population

Use FBL3N, supplementing with SE16N on BKPF and BSEG when needed. Retain selection criteria, raw extracts, company code, dates and population reconciliation.

3. Trace workflow and posting

Inspect selected documents in FB03, and use SWI1 to compare independent approver and approval timestamps against posting timestamps. Obtain workflow routing configuration and its change history.

4. Investigate the threshold mismatch

Thornfield case: policy threshold ten thousand dollars, configured threshold one hundred thousand dollars from January third to June sixth. Four hundred twelve entries totalling eighteen point four million dollars were not routed. A clean sample of twenty five routed entries did not test the excluded population.

5. Conclude and prepare evidence

Conclude the routing configuration did not reflect policy. Correct the threshold, retest the previously unrouted population and measure approval to posting gaps. Preserve FBL3N selection, FB03 header, SWI1 log, threshold history and gap calculation. A clean sample cannot rescue a flawed population.
