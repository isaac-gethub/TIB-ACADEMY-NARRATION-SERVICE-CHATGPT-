Lab 17 — The Leaver

Five instructional sections. Browser narration; recorded MP3s pending. Open interactive lab

1. Population

Define the test population from the HR termination list, not the IT deprovisioning queue.

2. Account status

Inspect terminated users in SU01 for lock status, validity dates and user type.

3. Logon history

Use SUIM or RSUSR200 to compare last logon dates with recorded termination dates.

4. Exceptions

Keep two exception columns: accounts still active and accounts with post termination logons. Do not combine the counts.

5. Conclusion

Report delayed access removal and post termination use. A proposed retrospective HR record correction is not sufficient evidence of timely removal.
