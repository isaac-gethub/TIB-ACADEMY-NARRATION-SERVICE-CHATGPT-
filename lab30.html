Lab 30 — The Early Invoice

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Population

Twenty two billing documents were created in the final three business days of the quarter. Ten were sampled.

2. Delivery trace

Trace each sampled invoice to its delivery and actual goods issue timestamp in VL03N.

3. Timing

Four of ten invoices were created before goods issue, from twenty five minutes to just over one day early.

4. Pattern

All four exceptions came from one regional sales office. Investigate revenue adjustments and prior quarter recurrence.

5. Conclusion

Recommend investigating the office and considering a system block on billing before completed goods issue.
