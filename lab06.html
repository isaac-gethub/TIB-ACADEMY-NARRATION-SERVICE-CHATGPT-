Lab 6 — The Other Ledger

Five instructional sections. Browser narration; recorded MP3s pending. Open interactive lab

1. Control definition

Define whether the acquired entity's journal review is system enforced or manual. Obtain controller confirmation and document the nine-month period.

2. Monthly evidence

Request the full nine months of reviewer sign-off emails. In the case, six months have evidence and three do not.

3. Journal testing

Trace twenty five manual journal entries to review evidence. All twenty five from evidenced months passed; this does not prove operation in the other three months.

4. Gap analysis

Record the separate design issue of no system enforcement and the operating evidence gap for three months. No reported issues does not prove review occurred.

5. Conclusion

Recommend system-enforced approval before group SAP migration and retrospective testing for the three unevidenced months. Index the controller confirmation, sign-off population and journal sample.
