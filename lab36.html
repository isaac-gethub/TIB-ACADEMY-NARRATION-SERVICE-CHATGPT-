Lab 36 — The Map Before the Testing

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Walkthroughs

Walkthrough interviews with the Controller, AP Manager and Treasury lead establish the Procure to Pay and Record to Report control map.

2. Control classification

Fourteen key controls were identified. Two originally described as automated had no confirmed configuration and were reclassified manual.

3. Testing approach

Invoice tolerance was reclassified as a key control and its testing approach changed to full population analytics.

4. Entity scope

Control owner review revealed bank detail workflow had not reached two recently acquired company codes.

5. Conclusion

Validate the scope gap before fieldwork, secure control owner sign off, and maintain the RCM as a living testing document.
