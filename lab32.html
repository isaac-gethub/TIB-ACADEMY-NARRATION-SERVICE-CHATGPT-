Lab 32 — The Rate That Didn't Update

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Rate change

The tax code rate changed from seven point five to eight point two five percent, effective March first.

2. Transport timing

Transport history confirms production go live on March fifteenth, fourteen days late.

3. Affected transactions

Four hundred twelve transactions posted at the old rate between March first and fourteenth.

4. Tax impact

BSET recomputation identifies thirty eight thousand six hundred dollars of under collected tax.

5. Conclusion

Require Tax and Basis to jointly confirm go live, and refer the shortfall to Tax for correction or disclosure assessment.
