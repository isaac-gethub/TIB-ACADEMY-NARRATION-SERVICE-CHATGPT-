Lab 31 — The Uncleared Item

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Population

At quarter end, FAGLL03 shows 146 open clearing items. Twelve are older than the thirty day policy.

2. Aging

The oldest item is ninety seven days outstanding. Reconcile the full population before sampling.

3. Investigation

FEBAN shows no documented investigation notes for nine of the twelve aged items.

4. Scope

Three aged items relate to Ops Account Four, an active bank account omitted from reconciliation scope for five months.

5. Conclusion

Restore the active account to scope, investigate the backlog and review the configuration change record.
