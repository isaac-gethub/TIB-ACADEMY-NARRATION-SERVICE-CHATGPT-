Lab 11 — The Comment Letter

Five instructional sections. Browser narration; recorded MP3s pending. Open interactive lab

1. Scope

Lab 11. The Comment Letter. Verify evidence for an S E C comment response is complete and organized for twelve months and four referenced controls within a ten day response window.

2. Evidence index

Collect the full unedited evidence population. Index it by month and by control, preserving the source, owner, period and file references for Senior Lead and Legal review.

3. Early escalation

During assembly, supporting documentation for month seven was missing. Escalate the gap to the Senior Lead on day two of the ten day response window rather than hiding it until the deadline.

4. Recovery

Find the missing month's records in the archived mailbox. Validate the recovered documentation, retain provenance and update the evidence index before submission.

5. Conclusion

Conclude that the month seven gap was identified early and resolved before submission. Recommend a standing evidence retention checklist for S E C reportable controls so missing records are detected before a comment letter arrives.
