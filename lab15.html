Lab 15 — The Risk Register

Five instructional sections. Browser narration; recorded MP3s pending. Open interactive lab

1. Scope

Confirm every risk marked mitigated in the enterprise risk register links to a real active tested control.

2. Reconciliation

Export all sixty four mitigated risks and reconcile their linked control IDs to the current control inventory.

3. Exceptions

Fifty eight references resolved. Three referred to retired controls and three referred to IDs that never existed.

4. Risk assessment

Assess the six underlying risks and any genuine replacement controls. Treat the six as unmitigated pending reassessment.

5. Conclusion

Conclude six of sixty four mitigation claims lacked valid current controls. Require risk register updates whenever controls are retired.
