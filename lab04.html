4. Investigate exceptions

In the fictional Thornfield case, twenty new-hire requests were sampled. Eighteen matched current role design, but two were provisioned with a legacy composite role retired six months earlier. Conclude that the new-hire provisioning control did not operate for two of twenty sampled requests.
