Lab 23 — The Job That Stopped Running

Five instructional sections. Browser narration; recorded MP3s pending. Open interactive lab

1. Expected schedule

A daily batch job across ninety days creates ninety expected executions. Establish this before reviewing results.

2. Initial filter

An SM37 report with Cancelled unticked can look clean while hiding failures.

3. Cancelled jobs

With Cancelled included, the case identifies eighty four completed and six cancelled job executions.

4. Missing executions

SM36 reveals a separate four day gap when a configuration change left the job not re released. A job that never runs creates no failed execution row.

5. Conclusion

Recommend monitoring expected versus actual job schedules, including every status and missing execution dates.
