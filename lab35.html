Lab 35 — The Recommendation Nobody Checked

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Approval evidence

Compare access approval decisions with the Joule recommendation shown at decision time.

2. Fast matches

Five of seven sampled approvals matched Joule and took under five seconds each.

3. Escalations

The other two decisions took several minutes, differed from Joule, and were escalated to Security.

4. Rule governance

A Joule modified continuous controls monitoring rule went live without separate documented human sign off.

5. Conclusion

Require documented independent review and a human approval gate for AI modified monitoring rules. Test the wider population before generalizing.
