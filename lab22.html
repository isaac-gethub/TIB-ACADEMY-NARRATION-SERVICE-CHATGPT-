Lab 22 — The Indirect Access

Five instructional sections. Browser narration; recorded MP3s pending. Open interactive lab

1. RFC inventory

Review SM59 for active RFC connections from external systems. The case identifies twelve connections.

2. Volume

Use ST03N and SE16N to quantify transactions. Three connections each exceed five hundred transactions per day.

3. Classification

Review USMM classification. The three material connections are classified as technical or background users.

4. Transaction content

Their transaction content includes order creation and invoice posting, indicating genuine business activity through the interfaces.

5. Escalation

Record potential indirect access exposure and escalate for formal review by IT asset management, Legal and licensing counsel. Do not determine liability from the technical test alone.
