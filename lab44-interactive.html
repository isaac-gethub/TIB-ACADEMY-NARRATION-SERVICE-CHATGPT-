Advanced controls testing — Financial configuration change control

Scenario: A rate configuration update lacks approval evidence.

  1. Establish the change population and effective dates.
  2. Trace the change to its request, approver and implementer.
  3. Test authorization and independent review evidence.
  4. Record the exception, corrective action and retest.

Required evidence: Change-control test record and exception conclusion.

Assessment: Explain the test population, supporting evidence, exception decision and retest method.
