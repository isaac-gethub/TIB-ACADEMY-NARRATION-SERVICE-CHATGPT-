Lab 45 — The Off-Cycle Exception

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Population

Start with all twenty two off cycle payroll runs in the quarter, covering bonuses, corrections and a severance payment.

2. Approval segregation

Twenty one runs have separate requester and approver identities. One bonus correction shows the same person in both roles.

3. Business justification

Eight sampled payments agree with their documented HR and Finance justifications within immaterial rounding. This does not cure the segregation exception.

4. Trend analysis

Off cycle payment volume rose from two point one percent to six point four percent of total payroll over the trailing four quarters. The cause has not yet been established.

5. Conclusion

Recommend a second approver above a materiality threshold and open a separate investigation into whether regular payroll process problems or legitimate business events caused the rise.
