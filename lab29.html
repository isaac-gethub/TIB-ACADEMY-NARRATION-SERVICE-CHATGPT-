Lab 29 — The Held Order

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Population

Thirty eight sales orders were credit blocked in the test period. Nine releases were sampled.

2. Release history

VKM4 release history identifies who released each held order and when.

3. Independence

Six of nine sampled releases were performed by the same sales representative who created the order.

4. Justification

The six same-person releases lacked documented justification. Three independent releases had manager approval emails.

5. Conclusion

Recommend separating order creation and release authority and requiring a documented release reason. Review role design across teams.
