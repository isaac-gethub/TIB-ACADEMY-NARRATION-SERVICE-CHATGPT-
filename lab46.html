Lab 46 — The Number That Didn't Match

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Payroll population

Review Thornfield's Q3 payroll posting summary. Gross pay is four million one hundred eighty two thousand six hundred dollars, combined deductions and employer costs one million one hundred ninety six thousand four hundred dollars, and net pay two million nine hundred eighty six thousand two hundred dollars.

2. GL balance extraction

Extract payroll expense and liability general ledger balances for the same Q3 period using read-only SE16N reporting.

3. Reconciliation

Gross pay and employer cost postings reconcile, but the payroll liability account shows a thirty eight thousand four hundred dollar shortfall.

4. FB03 adjustment review

Trace the shortfall to a quarter end manual adjustment reducing the payroll liability. FB03 review identifies the Payroll Manager as the poster, with no second approval on file.

5. Conclusion

Request the adjustment rationale and check prior quarters for similar entries. Recommend independent approval of manual payroll general ledger adjustments above a low materiality threshold, including at period end.
