Lab 24 — The Match That Wasn't

Five instructional sections. Browser narration; recorded MP3s pending. Open interactive lab

1. Configuration

Test three way matching at every plant, not only the control matrix. Eighteen of twenty two plants use three way match; four acquired plants use two way match.

2. Invoice sample

Review twenty invoices from the four two way plants. Three had goods receipts posted after invoice payment.

3. Receipt verification

Material document evidence confirms those three receipts were genuine, indicating a timing issue rather than proven fraud.

4. GR/IR aging

At three way plants, nine purchase orders had goods receipts uncleared for more than one hundred eighty days. Three manual MR11 clearings lacked supporting approval.

5. Conclusion

Recommend bringing the four acquired plants onto three way match and separately requiring approval evidence for manual GR IR clearing.
