Lab 8 — The Third Party

Five instructional sections. Browser narration; recorded MP3s pending. Open interactive lab

1. Control and contract scope

Lab 8. The Third Party. Confirm changes executed by a managed services vendor have the same required approval and evidence trail as internal changes. Define the period, vendor service accounts and contractual reporting requirements.

2. Independent SAP transport population

Use S T M S import history and S M twenty security audit logs to independently extract vendor executed transports. In the Thornfield training case, SAP showed thirty one transports under the vendor service account.

3. Vendor reporting reconciliation

Obtain the vendor change log for the same period. The vendor reported twenty seven transports. Reconcile transport identifiers and investigate four omissions, which the vendor classified as routine maintenance.

4. Approval testing

Independently match vendor activity to Thornfield's change approval records. All thirty one transports had valid C A B tickets. Check approval and migration timestamps and emergency justification windows rather than treating a ticket number as sufficient proof.

5. Conclusion and contract follow-up

Conclude the vendor log understated actual activity by four transports, but independent SAP evidence confirmed all thirty one were approved. Require reporting definitions that prohibit unilateral exclusions, and refer the contract wording issue to Legal and Procurement.
