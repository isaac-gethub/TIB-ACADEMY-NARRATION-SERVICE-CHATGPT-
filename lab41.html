Lab 41 — The Hedge That Wasn't Documented

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Hedge population

Start with the Hedge Management Cockpit population of fourteen active foreign exchange hedges for the quarter.

2. Designation timestamps

Twelve designation memos were timely. Two were created nine and fourteen days after trade inception.

3. Effectiveness evidence

Review prospective effectiveness testing at designation and retrospective testing for subsequent periods across all fourteen hedges.

4. Accounting reconciliation

Three sampled financial accounting postings matched their documented cash flow hedge treatment. Correct posting evidence does not resolve late designation.

5. Conclusion

Add a designation memo deadline to the hedge workflow, and ask Accounting to assess whether the two late designations require adjustments under the applicable reporting rules.
