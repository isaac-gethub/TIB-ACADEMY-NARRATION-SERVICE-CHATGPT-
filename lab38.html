Lab 38 — How Many Is Enough

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Frequency and sizing

Use each control's operating frequency and engagement reliance strategy to justify its sample size.

2. Book 3 population defect

Book three used twenty five entries, a suitable size for a frequently operating control, but sourced them from a workflow filtered report instead of independent FBL3N or BKPF data.

3. Book 27 method mismatch

Book twenty seven sampled twenty five invoices from a population of forty one thousand eight hundred forty two; later full population analysis showed a better method for this risk.

4. Aligned examples

Books five and sixteen had appropriate sizing and independently sourced populations.

5. Conclusion

Treat sample size and population sourcing as separate decisions. Document the source verification explicitly before selecting items.
