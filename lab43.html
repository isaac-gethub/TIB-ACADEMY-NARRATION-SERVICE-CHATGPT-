Lab 43 — The Employee Who Wasn't

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Employee population

Extract the active payroll employee population of one thousand eight hundred forty two, and examine Infotype zero zero zero nine bank details.

2. Duplicate analysis

Three bank account numbers each appear on two employee IDs. Two of those pairs also share an address in Infotype zero zero zero six.

3. Independent verification

Inspect flagged employee master records in PA20 and seek confirmation from the appropriate cost center managers.

4. Exception evaluation

One pair are real siblings sharing a bank account and address. Another pair contains an unconfirmed identity with fourteen biweekly paychecks and no timesheet or badge evidence on file.

5. Conclusion

Escalate the unresolved identity to Internal Audit and HR Security, calculate the total paid amount and investigate recovery options. Shared banking details alone do not prove fraud.
