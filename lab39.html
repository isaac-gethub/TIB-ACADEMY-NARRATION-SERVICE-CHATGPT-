Lab 39 — What Kind of Finding Is This

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Finding inventory

Build a complete inventory of the engagement's findings, including workflow thresholds, bank details, credit release design and tax rate timing.

2. Material weakness

The workflow threshold issue exposed eighteen point four million dollars across four hundred twelve entries, with no compensating control, and is classified as a material weakness in the case.

3. Significant deficiency

Three of thirty eight bank detail changes were unrouted in two acquired company codes. The case classifies this as a significant deficiency based on a manual compensating review.

4. Deficiency

The tax rate transport gap resulted in thirty eight thousand six hundred dollars under collected with no identified recurrence pattern, and is classified as a deficiency.

5. Conclusion

Document severity using quantitative impact, pervasiveness and compensating controls. Verify the manual review's effectiveness before relying on it.
