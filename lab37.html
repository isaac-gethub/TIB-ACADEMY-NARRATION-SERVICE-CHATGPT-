Lab 37 — The Control That Was Never Built to Hold

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Control objective

The risk and control matrix requires credit-block release by someone independent of the order creator.

2. Role inspection

In PFCG, the standard sales representative role combines order entry and credit release authorization.

3. RACI mapping

A dedicated credit analyst role exists but is not used in the current sales process.

4. Prior operating evidence

Lab twenty nine found six of nine credit releases were performed by the order creator, consistent with this design gap.

5. Conclusion

Redesign release responsibility around independent credit analysts, verify assignments, and only then retest operating effectiveness.
