Lab 27 — The Tolerance That Swallowed It

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Settings

OMR6 shows an absolute invoice tolerance unchanged since go-live despite commodity price increases.

2. Variances

RSEG analysis finds 41 invoices at 92 to 99 percent of the absolute limit.

3. Vendor pattern

Twelve near-limit invoices come from two vendors, requiring further review.

4. Blocked invoices

MRBR shows no blocked invoices, so the release-independence test is not applicable for this period.

5. Conclusion

Review tolerance settings against current prices and investigate the concentrated near-limit transactions.
