Scenario: A Firefighter session has an incomplete review trail.

  1. Reconcile approved access windows to the session inventory.
  2. Match transaction logs to controller reviews and timestamps.
  3. Identify missing logs, late reviews and unapproved activity.
  4. Record the exception, control owner and retest evidence.

Required evidence: Session-to-log reconciliation and controller review record.

Assessment: Explain the test population, supporting evidence, exception decision and retest method.
