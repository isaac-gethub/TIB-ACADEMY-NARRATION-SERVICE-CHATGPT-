1. Investigation scope

Lab 9. The Tip Line. Independently reconstruct a journal entry flagged by an anonymous tip. Define the document, period and scope before reviewing the original tester's conclusion.

2. Ledger and change history

Use F B L three N, S E sixteen N with B K P F and B S E G, and F B zero three to inspect ledger entries and document history. In the case two changes occurred after posting: an amount adjustment and a cost center change. Neither appeared in the original workpaper.

3. Independent source data

Obtain the underlying data directly from the source owner rather than relying on the original preparer's summary spreadsheet. Record the source request, provenance and evidence trail for potential Internal Audit, External Audit or Legal review.

4. Recompute estimate

Independently recompute the estimate using source data where possible. The result differed from the booked amount by fourteen percent, outside the stated reasonable range. Document assumptions and do not infer intent from the variance.

5. Conclusion and escalation

Conclude that the independent reconstruction identified two undisclosed post-posting changes and a fourteen percent variance. Escalate to Internal Audit for reportability determination, with Legal as appropriate. Require underlying source data as standard support going forward.
