Lab 44 — The Rate Nobody Signed Off

Five-part browser narrated lesson.

1. Change population

Extract all forty seven Basic Pay Infotype zero zero zero eight changes for the quarter, including old and new values, the changer and the timestamp.

2. Approval matching

Compare every change against independent HR workflow approvals. Forty four have complete approvals; three have no approval record.

3. Financial impact

Calculate the financial impact of the three exceptions using rate differences and the affected payroll periods. Do not assume a dollar total without source payroll data.

4. Role design

All three exceptions were made by the same HR administrator. PFCG review shows that administrator's role permits both changing and approving Basic Pay.

5. Conclusion

The underlying issue is role design, not three unrelated omissions. Separate change and approval privileges and check whether other HR administrator roles carry the same conflict.
