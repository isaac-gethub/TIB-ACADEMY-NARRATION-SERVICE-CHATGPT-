Lab 19 — The Interface Gap

Five instructional sections. Browser narration; recorded MP3s pending. Open interactive lab

1. Assertion

Define completeness and accuracy of Warehouse Management System goods movements transferred into SAP.

2. Queue evidence

Obtain the source transmission count first, then inspect SM58, SMQ1 and SMQ2 for errors and aged items.

3. IDoc errors

Use WE02 statuses fifty one and sixty four to identify IDoc errors during the quarter.

4. Reconciliation

Compare forty one thousand two hundred six source movements to forty one thousand one hundred ninety two SAP postings, a gap of fourteen.

5. Conclusion

The gap is concentrated, not scattered. Investigate its value and composition, plus aged queue failures, rather than dismissing it on percentage alone.
